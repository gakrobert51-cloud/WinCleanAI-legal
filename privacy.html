WinCleanAI Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026
Last updated: 18 November 2026
Controller: Robert Gak

1. Scope and summary

WinCleanAI is a Windows 11 cleanup utility distributed through Microsoft Store. It includes the desktop application, the cleanup engine, and the winclean command-line tool (together, the “App”). This policy explains how Robert Gak (“WinCleanAI”, “we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the App and this website. It does not cover Microsoft’s own handling of Store account, installation, update, or crash data; Microsoft’s privacy statement applies to those services.

WinCleanAI is designed to work entirely on your device:

  • The App works offline. Scanning, policy decisions, quarantine, restore, audit verification, and cleanup do not require a network connection.
  • We do not collect, receive, store, sell, or share personal data through the App.
  • The App contains no telemetry, analytics, advertising, crash-reporting SDKs, cloud AI, or remote file upload.
  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • Microsoft handles Store downloads, installations, updates, and any Store-related diagnostic data under its own privacy statement.

Because the App does not send us data, there is no server-side WinCleanAI account, profile, or database about you.

2. Data processed locally on your device

The App processes the following data locally to perform its functions. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party.

Data Purpose Location
File metadata: name, path, size, timestamps, attributes, hashes Scan, policy decision, and cleanup preview Your device
Quarantine payload and metadata Recoverability and verification %LOCALAPPDATA%\WinCleanAI\Quarantine
Audit records: actor SID, operation, path, decision, hashes Tamper-evident record of cleanup actions %LOCALAPPDATA%\WinCleanAI\Logs
Audit HMAC key Audit integrity verification DPAPI CurrentUser; same directory
Settings and cleanup-zone configuration App behavior Your device
Engine log Start-up and connection diagnostics %LOCALAPPDATA%\WinCleanAI\Logs\engine.log

The audit log is a local operational record. It can contain personal data such as your Windows user SID and file paths. It stays on your device. In the Store (MSIX) build, the App and engine run as the signed-in standard user, and local data is access-controlled to SYSTEM and the current user.

Uninstalling the App intentionally leaves quarantine and audit data in place so that a reinstall can recover it. You can remove the data at any time by deleting %LOCALAPPDATA%\WinCleanAI or by using the App or CLI.

3. No telemetry, analytics, or cloud processing

The App does not:

  • send usage statistics, crash reports, or diagnostic logs to us;
  • use advertising or analytics SDKs;
  • call a cloud AI service;
  • upload file names, paths, hashes, or file content;
  • perform update checks (Microsoft Store delivers updates).

Cleanup decisions are made by a deterministic policy engine on your device. A future version may add optional local analysis features; this policy and the App will be updated before any such feature is enabled.

4. Network access

The core features do not require network access. The App may use the network only when you explicitly choose to:

  • open a mailto: support link, in which case your email client handles the message;
  • open a web link in this policy or the Store listing, in which case your browser handles it.

Microsoft Store itself may use the network to install or update the App and may collect standard installation and crash information under Microsoft’s privacy statement.

9. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only:

  • with Microsoft, for Store distribution, certification, installation, updates, and Store-related reporting under Microsoft’s terms;
  • with our email provider, Google, when you contact support;
  • with authorities or professional advisers when required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We do not share local file metadata, quarantine payloads, or audit records with advertisers, data brokers, or AI providers.

10. Security

We use Microsoft Store package signing and distribution, NTFS access controls for local data, DPAPI for the audit key, and TLS for email and this website. The audit log is tamper-evident through a hash chain and HMAC. In standard-user mode, the same Windows user could technically rewrite local files, so the audit log is an operational record rather than a defense against the device owner. No system is perfectly secure. If you discover a vulnerability, contact robertgak18@gmail.com.

11. Children

WinCleanAI is a professional utility and is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent). We do not knowingly collect children’s data.
